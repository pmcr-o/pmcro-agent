Do not manufacture an error. Do not repair the defect in the same cycle. Produce evidence, an independent CHECK verdict, one earned constraint, and exactly one unexecuted next seed.

## Bounds

- Read-only discovery for the first cycle unless a registered capability and grant explicitly authorize mutation.
- Do not widen authority.
- Do not create a Grok-specific marketplace, lifecycle, queue, or trail format.
- Do not use private chain-of-thought as evidence; summarize observable reasoning/evidence only.
- Do not merge to `main`.
- Prefer a genuine LOOP/HALT over an easy PASS when evidence supports a defect, but never force a failure.

- ORCHESTRATE: accepted/denied intent, resolved route, inherited authority ceiling, I-context identity.
- PLAN: smallest evidence path.
- MAKE: repository evidence references only.
- CHECK: PASS / LOOP / HALT with why the evidence meets that verdict.
- REFLECT: exactly one earned constraint + exactly one unexecuted next seed.
